# FlowSentinel design contract

## Thesis
A one-person fund desk that turns SoSoValue ETF flows and news into a confirmed SoDEX testnet order — refuses neon trading-dashboard sprawl.

## World
Dark charcoal institutional terminal (SoDEX ink #0A0A0A / #171717 / #262626) with SoSoValue flame orange (#FF4F20) as the rare committed accent, SoDEX warm orange (#FF7637 / #FD7537) for primary actions, pueblo (#7F2711 / #994721) for deep tinted surfaces. Neutrals from SoDEX greys (#A3A3A3, #D4D4D4). Success #1EA858. No purple washes, no neon glow edges, no orange-blue gradients.

## Story
Data → Thesis → Risk gate → Confirm → Execute → Track record.

## First viewport
Daily thesis hero with confidence ring, FlowRadar signature (ETF net-flow aperture), live/fallback badges on every panel, risk gate strip, confirm CTA — no wallet gate.

## Signature
FlowRadar: three concentric arcs sharing a centre (the sentinel aperture) that opens with net ETF inflow magnitude; a radial tick shows flow direction. Not a shield, bolt, or coin.

## Risk
Must not read as generic crypto neon orange UI — restraint, hairline borders, soft elevation, one accent dosage.
