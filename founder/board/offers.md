# Offers lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- Clear dream outcome for the buyer: trade on SoDEX with simpler onboarding, visible costs and pre-trade safety checks.
- Effort is low: free to use, one-time fee approval, installable PWA, mobile wallet support.
- Safety tools never promise profit, so the claim is honest and checkable.
- Cheap-to-deliver stack already exists: trade check, position size, portfolio health, journal, alerts, brief, plus news/ETF/sector data.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. Likelihood: nothing is verified. No real mainnet trade, WalletConnect relay or Gemini call yet. The buyer has no proof it works, and neither does the founder.
2. Market and reach: the segment is unvalidated. Pain, spending power, growth and a way to reach SoDEX/ValueChain traders are all open questions. No marketing budget is stated.
3. Commodity risk: SoDEX's own site and sodexextra.xyz bots sell the same swap. If the only difference is a fee the user must approve, the offer is a cost, not a benefit. Nothing in the brief explains why a trader would choose this front end.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many SoDEX users exist, and how many trade spot actively? Source?
- Who are the first 20 users, by name or channel, and how do they find the app?
- What does a trader lose today without the safety checks? Any example of a bad fill the tool would have caught?
- What is the guarantee? E.g. a pre-trade warning is shown, or the fee is refunded on a flagged bad fill.
- Would users pay 3 bp when the native front end charges what? (Open: native fee level.)
- Does the Builder Race reward (ranked by fees and authorized users) align with user benefit, or only with your ranking?
- How long from first visit to first completed swap?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Complete one real mainnet trade, one real WalletConnect session and one real Gemini call, with evidence.
- Name the segment and show a count of reachable users.
- Give a stated reason to choose this over SoDEX's own site, with a concrete guarantee.
- Get 10 real users to complete a swap before further build.
