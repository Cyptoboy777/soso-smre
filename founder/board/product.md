# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The safety checks are a clear stance: they flag book depth, slippage, size and concentration, and never promise profit. That restraint is a product choice, and it is the right one.
- Non-custodial with keys server-side, one-time fee approval and clearer costs address real friction in onboarding to SoDEX.
- Starting from "pre-trade safety" gives a possible single idea to build around.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. No single thing. The brief lists a trading interface, news/ETF/sector data, and six Gemini skills (trade check, position size, portfolio health, journal, alerts, brief). That is a terminal, a research feed and a coach in one. Nothing says which of these must be brilliant. The Product lens would cut to one.
2. The core experience is unproven. It has not been verified on a real mainnet trade, a real WalletConnect relay or a real Gemini call. The first five minutes (connect wallet, check a trade, swap) may break at exactly these seams: wallet, relay, model, order book, fee approval.
3. The product may not feel better than the alternatives. SoDEX's own site and sodexextra.xyz bots already exist. If the swap itself feels the same, the safety layer is the only difference, and it must be felt on the first trade.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what is SoSwapDEX?
- Walk me through the first five minutes of a new user, tap by tap. Where is the first moment they feel the product is better?
- Which of the six skills would you cut first, and which one survives?
- Where does the experience pass between systems (wallet, WalletConnect, Gemini, SoDEX, SoSoValue), and what does the user see when each one fails?
- What does the user see about the 3 bp fee before approving it?
- Who is the exact user? Open question; the brief says the segment is still to be validated.
- How many trades have you made yourself on it?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Name the one thing and demote or remove the rest from the main flow.
- Complete a real mainnet trade, a real WalletConnect session and a real Gemini call end to end, and record the failures.
- Show five minutes of first-time use with a real person who is not the builder, and report what they stumbled on.
- Show the fee and the safety result plainly before every swap.
